For Universities

Give your engineering and education students hands-on leadership experience by starting an SLCCE chapter.

Why Found a Chapter

A chapter gives your students real teaching and leadership experience, planning curriculum, running classrooms, and mentoring younger students, while extending SLCCE's reach into a new community. It's an especially strong fit for engineering and education colleges looking to give students hands-on, resume-building impact.

Leadership Experience
Students plan and lead real workshops from day one, not just observe.
Cross-Disciplinary
Built for engineering students and education students to lead together.
A National Network
New chapters get curriculum, training, and support from every chapter before them.

How It Works

1
Recruit a founding team of student volunteers.
2
Partner with a local middle or high school.
3
Run your first workshop within a semester.
4
Get ongoing curriculum, training, and support from SLCCE.
