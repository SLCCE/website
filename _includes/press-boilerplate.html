About SLCCE

Students Leading Collaborative Computing Education (SLCCE) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that mobilizes university students to deliver free Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering workshops to under-resourced middle and high school students. Founded in 2024 at North Carolina State University, SLCCE's chapter model pairs undergraduate volunteers with partner schools that lack a CS or engineering curriculum. Learn more at slcce-inc.org.
