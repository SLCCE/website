Our Impact

Every dollar donated goes directly toward workshop materials, volunteer training, and travel, the things that let our flagship chapter say yes to a school regardless of its budget. That support has already reached over 400 students, with early funding support from partners including DigiKey.

What's Next

Your gift helps us grow beyond our single flagship chapter, expand our open-source workshop library, and reach more classrooms next year than we did this one, part of the five-year goal to establish chapters across at least three regions nationwide.
