For Teachers

Free, curriculum-aligned CSC & ECE workshops delivered to your classroom, no cost, no setup required.

How Workshops Work

Our volunteers arrive with everything they need, lesson plans, materials, and mentors, and run the workshop in your classroom while you stay on as the teacher of record. Sessions are designed to complement your existing STEM curriculum, not replace it, and require no advance prep on your end.

Workshops We Currently Offer

ECE
Introduction to Circuits
Hands-on breadboard work covering voltage, current, and basic components.
CSC
Introduction to Programming
A first hands-on look at code, logic, and problem-solving.
ECE / CSC
Robotics Fundamentals
Sensors, actuators, and simple robotics projects built as a team.

Trust & Safety

Every workshop is led by trained, background-checked university volunteers, with a teacher present in the room throughout. Materials are shared and approved with your school ahead of time, nothing arrives unannounced.
