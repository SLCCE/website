SLCCE at NC State is now scheduling free hands-on workshops for the spring 2027 semester. Middle and high schools in the Triangle, especially those without a CS or engineering curriculum, are encouraged to reach out.

## What's included

- A workshop in electrical engineering (circuits, sensors, microcontrollers), computer science (programming and robotics), or both
- All equipment and materials, brought by our volunteers
- A college-pathways conversation with undergraduate engineers and computer scientists
