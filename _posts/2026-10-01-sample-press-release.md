Students Leading Collaborative Computing Education (SLCCE), a student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit, has now delivered free, hands-on Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering workshops to more than 400 middle and high school students through its founding chapter at North Carolina State University.

Since its first workshop with Trinity Christian School in March 2025, the chapter has partnered with Reach for the STARS, MSEN, and NCBCE. Undergraduate volunteers teach circuits, soldering, microcontrollers, and introductory programming on real equipment that many students rarely see before college.

The chapter's model of recruiting undergraduate volunteers, partnering with nearby schools, adapting a shared curriculum, and reporting outcomes is the template for SLCCE's planned expansion to new campuses under its five-year strategic plan.

Schools and teachers interested in hosting a workshop, and university students interested in starting a chapter, can learn more at slcce-inc.org.
