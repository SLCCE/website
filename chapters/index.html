SLCCE Chapters

Every SLCCE workshop is run by a university chapter, undergraduate engineers and computer scientists who design the curriculum, teach the sessions, and build the relationship with a partner school.

Active Chapters

We currently have one chapter. It is the model we're using to launch the next ones.

SLCCE at NC State
Our founding chapter, a registered student organization at North Carolina State University. Volunteers studying electrical and computer engineering and computer science have delivered hands-on workshops to more than 400 middle and high school students across the Triangle.

What a Chapter Does

1
Recruits undergraduate volunteers from engineering and computing majors.
2
Partners with nearby middle and high schools that lack a CS or engineering curriculum.
3
Adapts SLCCE's hands-on workshop curriculum to the partner school's schedule and grade level.
4
Runs the workshops, tracks outcomes, and reports back so the curriculum improves for every chapter.

Bridging the gap in STEM education for under-resourced middle and high school students nationwide.
