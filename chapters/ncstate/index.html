SLCCE at NC State

Our founding chapter. Undergraduate engineers and computer scientists at North Carolina State University who design and teach hands-on computing workshops for nearby middle and high schools.

SLCCE at NC State is a registered student organization. It is not an agency of NC State, and the views and activities of the organization are its own and are not endorsed by NC State.

What We Do

Hardware
Electrical Engineering
Circuits, soldering, microcontrollers, and sensors, taught on real equipment students rarely get to touch before college.
Software
Computer Science
Intro programming, robotics control, and project-based sessions where students build something that works by the end of the day.
Mentorship
Pathways to College
Volunteers talk candidly about applying to engineering programs, financial aid, and what the first year actually looks like.
