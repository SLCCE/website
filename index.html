Every student deserves a seat at the table in STEM.

Students Leading Collaborative Computing Education (SLCCE) mobilizes university students to deliver free Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering workshops to under-resourced middle and high schoolers, closing the opportunity gap one classroom at a time.

About SLCCE

A student-led nonprofit bringing free, hands-on CSC & ECE education to classrooms that would otherwise go without it, and building toward a future where every student has that access.
